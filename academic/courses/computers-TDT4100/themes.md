## Performance

### Right more often then not

A good chunk of performance enhancing concepts within computer architecture
rely on being right more often then not, as being wrong reduces the performance gain
of being right.

This can mean that a performance improving implementation might reduce performance if
its wrong x% amount of the time, depending on the implementation.

### Cahces

Normal memory is slow and far away, cahces are fast and close.

Many components of a cpu have their own close by cahces so they don't have
to store their state farther away
